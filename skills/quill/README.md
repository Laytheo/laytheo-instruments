# Quill

A method of formation that produces essays, worked with an agent over a note vault. It carries an essay through seven phases (capture and select, develop, witness the sources, compose and listen, review and adjudicate, verify and hand off, publish and QA). The agent brings ideas, structure, and sentences of its own, as proposals; the witnessing, the wrestling, and the deciding stay with you. State lives in a companion note (status, wireframe, section directives, source-quality ledger, review ledger, history), so a session can stop and resume cold.

**The companion is in flux.** The companion note described here is being reworked, and an upcoming release will change its shape.

## Make it yours

This is a starting point, not an install. It is one person's working method, written for an AI agent, and it is meant to be adapted. Give `SKILL.md` to your own agent and ask it to fit the method to you. Have it interview you first, on two fronts: the technical setup (where your notes live, and what tools it has to read and write them) and the method itself (how you actually keep notes, what you want to remember, what you believe and write from, what you want forming toward, and which parts of this practice fit you and which do not). Then let it rewrite the skill around your answers.

Once it is yours, bring a spark, a named essay, or a half-finished draft.

## What it assumes

- A markdown vault (Obsidian or similar) organized by the [Ontological Type System](https://laytheo.com/instruments/ontological-type-system), with a note tool your agent can call, for example an MCP server over your vault.
- The works your essays rest on, in a collection your agent can read, so quotations come from the text.
- A style guide note of your own, and optionally an editorial direction note. Without them the skill falls back to a short set of defaults.
- A publishing workflow of your own for the final phase; this skill hands off to it and does not replace it.
- The `witness` skill, which Quill calls in the Witness phase and at wrap-up.
